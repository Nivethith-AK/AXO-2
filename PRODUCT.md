# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Readers, web-novel fans, anime/manga enthusiasts, and listeners interested in dark fantasy, existential themes, and the creative universe of Δxolotl.

## Product Purpose
The official multimedia web destination for "Memento Mori: A Reason to Live" — a web-novel and chronicle by Δxolotl. It enables visitors to read chapter records, listen to original atmospheric soundtracks, and explore the lore of Adoniel Togami and Azrael.

## Positioning
An immersive celestial archive blending high-craft typography, interactive planetary orrery audio streaming, and seamless chapter reading.

## Operating Context
Modern desktop and mobile web viewports. Zero-dependency static architecture with instant load times and responsive layouts.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Dual reading modes: Standalone chapter page (`chapter.html`) and in-app reader overlay.
- Dual theme engine: Deep Space Dark Mode and Manuscript Light Mode, persistent in `localStorage`.
- Unified audio controller: Synchronized playback between top navigation toggle and rich soundtrack player.
- Pure vanilla stack: HTML5, CSS custom properties, and modern JavaScript.

## Brand Commitments
- Identity: Memento Mori // Δxolotl Presents // Project Eden.
- Visual Language: Celestial coordinates, sacred geometry orrery, and gold-tinted monochrome.
- Typographic integrity: Accurate Greek glyph preservation (e.g., Chapter α).

## Evidence on Hand
- Full Chapter α ("When We Met") and Chapter I ("A Death Wish") records in `js/chapters.js`.
- Master soundtrack: `assets/media/audio/To_Eternity_and_After.m4a`.
- Generative celestial canvas simulation in `js/canvas.js`.

## Product Principles
1. **Atmospheric Immersion:** Every visual and audio element reinforces the quiet devotion and celestial weight of the story.
2. **Uncompromising Legibility:** Generous line heights, readable contrast, and responsive layout across dark and light palettes.
3. **Harmonious Craft:** Precision borders, subtle gold accents, and fluid transitions that feel deliberate and refined.
