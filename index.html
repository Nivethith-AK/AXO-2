Three things worth knowing before you walk in.

Getting here

Metro to Little Tokyo, then eight minutes on foot. Lot C holds 400 cars and it fills by 19:00 on Saturday.

What to bring

ID, a sealed bottle under 500 ml, one soft bag under 30×20 cm. Chairs, umbrellas and pro cameras stay at home.

Safety and care

The welfare tent sits by the medical point, staffed all night. Step-free throughout, a quiet room next to Gate A, and a line you can text.
